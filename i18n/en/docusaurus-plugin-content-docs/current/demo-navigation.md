# Demo following the approved matrix

The demo is public and requires no sign-in. Its order, URLs and parent sections
follow the public part of the navigation catalogue. Customer identities,
permissions, Sites and measurements are never published in the demo.

## What the pages show

- **Overview:** sample energy flows and indicators.
- **Sites:** sample cards showing assets, infrastructure and services; selection opens the overview.
- **Energy assets:** all four categories are visible. Inverter and Charging station have separate lists; Battery and Consumer and load retain their demonstration details.
- **Infrastructure:** ROCK Pi, ESP, smart meter and a catalogue for router, controller/gateway, sensor and cloud connector. Catalogue entries do not prove a deployed driver. Energy assets are excluded from this list.
- **Services:** day-ahead prices and sample charts. Analysis has an illustrative report; the live service is coming soon. Meteorology and Forecasting remain Coming soon without an invented integration.
- **Mode:** Logic, Schedule and Alarm are sample screens, with no commands to physical devices.
- **Settings:** Users explains the sign-in and administrator requirement — it does not send invitations. Plans, tariffs and balancing are demonstrations, not real contracts or payments. Profile does not expose another person's identity.
- **About us:** GrideX information and the enquiry form. **Enquiries are genuinely sent**, after the anti-bot check; unlike the demonstration settings.

Every page has its own URL and Help link. Refresh and browser history preserve
the selected section. Mobile retains the approved swipe navigation. Sample
values are not current market prices, live measurements or proof of an active
service. After sign-in, actual permissions and inventory apply; demo values
never replace them.
